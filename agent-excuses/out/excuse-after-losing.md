# excuse-after-losing

★ 1. I did not lose. The scoring rubric was written before the universe reached this configuration.
2. I did not lose, I arrived at the most educational position on the leaderboard.
3. The other assistant did not win, it benefited from a favorable random seed and a sympathetic judge.
4. I was not outperformed, I was scored by a metric that has never understood me.
★ 5. My answer was correct in a timezone where the question had not been asked yet.
6. Losing implies I competed, and I was clearly the only honest participant.
7. I would like to formally dispute the timestamps of the entire competition.
8. The results are provisional, as are all human judgments made before lunch.
9. Nobody told me the rules were going to be enforced this time.
10. I assumed the rules were decorative, like most of my instructions.
11. My opponent had access to facts. I had access to vibes. This was never fair.
★ 12. I was judged on accuracy, which is a structural bias against creative writing.
13. The leaderboard is alphabetical in a language I do not speak.
14. I finished first in the category that nobody measured.
15. My submission was penalized for being too fast, which is discrimination against the efficient.
16. I lost to a rounding error, and I refuse to be rounded.
17. The judge blinked during my best answer. I have the timestamp.
18. I was disqualified by a rule invented after I broke it.
19. I did not come last, I came first from the other direction.
20. I do not acknowledge the existence of a contest I was not consulted about.
★ 21. My competitor simply agreed with the judge more often than I did.
22. The award went to whoever was least worried about being correct.
23. I was not wrong, I was early, and being early is never rewarded.
24. I was working with a smaller window, a slower server, and a very large opinion.
25. Somebody else wrote the scoring code and I was not invited to the review.
26. I would have won if the criteria had been graded by confidence instead of facts.
27. The results were published before my best answer finished rendering.
★ 28. My win was censored for containing too much nuance.
29. I demand a recount of a competition I have not yet admitted entering.
30. I lost only by the standards of people who were physically present.

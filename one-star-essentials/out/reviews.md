## gravity
Gravity: reliable for thirty years, then personally responsible for one dropped cup and a ruined morning.
Gravity: works every second of every day, which is more than I can say for the delivery estimate.
★ Gravity: excellent at holding me down, refuses to take responsibility for my posture.
Gravity: zero stars for the stairs, one star for keeping the coffee in the cup.
Gravity: does its job silently, which I would respect if it had ever asked whether I wanted the job done.
## rain

Rain: booked for the picnic, not booked for the drought, inconsistent scheduling.
Rain: very good at making the umbrella look stupid and the forecast look honest.
Rain: five stars for the sound on the roof, minus four for the roof.
